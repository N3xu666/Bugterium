# 05 Corporate and Business OSINT

Corporate intelligence is the systematic collection and analysis of publicly
available information about companies, their structure, finances,
partnerships, and operations.

> [!CAUTION]
> Authorized use only. All material is provided for educational purposes and authorized testing.<br>
> See [../../SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) for the full policy.

## Scope

This subcategory covers business-focused OSINT:

- **Corporate registries:** filings, ownership, directors.
- **Financial intelligence:** annual reports, SEC filings, sanctions lists.
- **Supply chain and vendor mapping:** public procurement, partner announcements.
- **Mergers and acquisitions:** press releases, regulatory filings.
- **Intellectual property:** patents, trademarks.
